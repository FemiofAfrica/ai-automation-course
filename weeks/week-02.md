## Key Concepts Covered

- Pipeline step 2 recap: context is the step you control most
- The five parts people confuse: instructions, context, memory, tools, retrieval
- The context window, context rot, and why more context is not better
- Context quality: relevant, current, consistent, specific
- Static context vs dynamic context, and where each belongs
- Knowledge base design: Markdown, folders, links, tags, YAML frontmatter, index or MOC, naming, source of truth, retrieval
- Tool comparison against current official sources: Obsidian, Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM), Notion, Logseq, plain folder
- Wiring the vault into a Claude project with a librarian instruction
- The correction loop: every fix becomes permanent context
- Context hygiene, privacy, stale and conflicting facts, permissions

## Deliverable

**Brand Context Vault**: a working Obsidian vault with an Index, Brand Voice Guide, Buyer Persona, Product Facts, Do-Not-Says and a Campaign Retro, wired into an AI project and proven with a before-and-after test on the same marketing task.
