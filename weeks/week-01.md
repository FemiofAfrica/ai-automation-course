# Week 1: Foundations of AI Automation

**Session Length:** ~3 hours
**Objective:** By the end of this session, you understand exactly what every AI term means, how the pieces fit together, and can map any marketing task to the right automation approach.

---

## Key Concepts Covered

- What is a Model? What is an LLM?
- What is an API? What is a Webhook?
- What is an AI Agent / Agent Harness?
- The Four-Layer Automation Stack (Chat, Workflow, Agent, Orchestration)
- Marketing Maturity Curve: where is your org?

## Deliverable

**Automation Readiness Map**: your entire marketing workload mapped against the AI stack with prioritized next steps.

## Homework

1. Get a Claude.ai account (Pro recommended, $20/mo)
2. Bring 3 real marketing challenges you'd like Claude to help with
3. Complete any gaps in your Automation Readiness Map
